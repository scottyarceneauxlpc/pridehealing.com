What to Expect

What a first session looks like

Reaching out is often the hardest part. Here’s exactly what happens once you do — so nothing about your first session is a mystery.

01

A free 15-minute consultation

Before anything is scheduled or billed, we have a short, no-pressure video call so you can hear my voice, ask questions, and decide whether this feels like a fit. If it isn’t, I’ll help point you toward someone who is.

02

Your first 60-minute session

We start with what brought you in — in your own words, at your own pace. There’s no clipboard interrogation; we simply talk, and I listen.

03

The practical parts, up front

Together we’ll walk through confidentiality and your rights under HIPAA, along with insurance, fees, and the cancellation policy — so there are no surprises later.

04

Getting to know you

As the conversation opens up, we’ll cover the background that matters — your history, family, health, and any prior counseling — only as deep as you’re comfortable going on day one.

05

A gentle check on safety

For every new client, I ask a few caring questions about your safety and well-being. It’s brief, it’s standard, and it’s asked with respect — not suspicion.

You leave the first session with a next appointment on the calendar (or a portal link to pick a time that works). No pressure — you’re always in charge of the pace.

Meet the Practice

Our Core Values

The meaning behind our name

Every letter of PRIDE represents a principle that guides how care is delivered at this practice — from the first consultation to every session that follows.

  • P

    Pride

    Who you are is worth celebrating. Every identity, story, and journey is welcomed here with respect — never judgment, never shame.

  • R

    Resilience

    Healing isn’t linear, and setbacks aren’t failures. Care here honors the strength it takes to keep showing up — one session, one day at a time.

  • I

    Inclusion

    Everyone deserves affirming care. This practice serves the LGBTQIA+ community and every other identity, background, and walk of life — across the Acadiana region and all of Louisiana.

  • D

    Dignity

    Struggle doesn’t erase your worth. From billing to boundaries to how you’re spoken to, respect is nonnegotiable — for every client, every time.

  • E

    Empowerment

    You are the expert on your own life. Therapy here provides tools and support so you can move forward with clarity, confidence, and a real path to recovery.

You Belong Here

A welcoming, safe space — for everyone

You are welcome here — exactly as you are. Whatever you're carrying, support exists, understanding exists, and acceptance exists, no matter your background, identity, or story.

01

Care for every population

Race, ethnicity, gender identity, sexual orientation, faith, age, or life experience — this practice is built to welcome you exactly as you are, without exception.

02

No shame. No judgment.

Pride Healing & Recovery exists to remove the shame so often attached to mental health and addiction — not to add to it. You deserve support, never demoralization.

03

Understanding & acceptance

Whatever brought you here, you'll be met with genuine respect and acceptance — because real healing starts with feeling safe enough to be honest.

04

Diversity, always welcome

Everyone deserves a counselor who sees them fully. This is a safe, affirming space for people of all populations and diversities across the Acadiana region and all of Louisiana.
