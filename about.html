About the Practice

Care built on 20+ years of showing up for people

Pride Healing & Recovery Services is an independent counseling practice founded to offer Louisiana communities a steady, respectful place to work through addiction, mental health struggles, and trauma.

Our Mission

Compassionate counseling built on Pride, Resilience, Inclusion, Dignity, and Empowerment — for every Louisianan.

Pride Healing & Recovery Services provides compassionate, evidence-based counseling for children ages 5 and up, adolescents, teens, and adults across the Acadiana region and throughout Louisiana — meeting each person where they are, honoring who they are, and walking with them toward recovery, mental wellness, and a life of dignity. We do this through five commitments, one for each letter of our name.

  • P

    Pride

    Who you are is worth celebrating. We welcome every identity, story, and journey with respect — never judgment, never shame.

  • R

    Resilience

    Healing is not linear, and setbacks are not failures. We honor the strength it takes to keep showing up — one session, one day at a time.

  • I

    Inclusion

    Everyone deserves affirming care. We serve the LGBTQIA+ community and every other identity, background, and walk of life — across the Acadiana region and all of Louisiana.

  • D

    Dignity

    Struggle does not erase your worth. From billing to boundaries to how you are spoken to, respect is nonnegotiable — for every client, every time.

  • E

    Empowerment

    You are the expert on your own life. We provide tools, evidence-based support, and steady presence so you can move forward with clarity, confidence, and a real path to recovery.

Your Counselor

Licensed, experienced, and personally invested

Scotty A. Arceneaux, MS, LPC, LAC, NCC, AADC

I became a counselor because I've watched people I care about struggle, and because I genuinely care about my community. Helping people heal isn't just a job to me — it's personal, and it's why I show up fully for every client who trusts me with their story.

I'm a native of Vermilion Parish, born and raised in Cajun Country here in South Louisiana, and I love how multicultural and genuinely unique our little corner of the world is. That upbringing is part of why I've devoted my life to helping people in need and being a voice for those who have never felt like they had one.

I bring over 20 years of experience supporting individuals and families through clinical counseling, assessment, and treatment planning as a Louisiana Licensed Professional Counselor (LPC) and Licensed Addiction Counselor (LAC), and I hold the National Certified Counselor (NCC) and Advanced Alcohol & Drug Counselor (AADC) credentials.

The therapeutic approach here is humanistic — rooted in the belief that every person already has what they need to grow, given empathy, genuine regard, and a relationship free of judgment. That means meeting you exactly where you are right now, not where a textbook says you should be. Every treatment plan is built individually around your specific history, culture, goals, and pace — never a generic, one-size-fits-all program applied the same way to everyone.

Our Approach

Respectful. Hopeful. Structured.

Every client's path looks different, but the approach stays the same: listen first, build a plan together, and provide the consistent structure that recovery and mental health progress require. Sessions are grounded in clinical assessment and treatment planning, with an emphasis on dignity and steady, sustainable progress — not quick fixes.

That also means never rushing you toward a version of yourself that isn't your own. Goals are set collaboratively, pace is set by you, and cultural background, identity, and personal values are treated as part of the plan — not obstacles to work around.

It also means your privacy is treated as non-negotiable. Every session, record, and conversation is protected under strict HIPAA and Louisiana confidentiality standards — what you share here stays between us.

The goal isn't just symptom relief — it's a healthier, more stable life you can sustain.

Our Commitment

Here to remove shame — never to add to it

Struggling with a mental health or substance use concern is not a character flaw, and no one is judged or demoralized here for needing support. Pride Healing & Recovery Services is a welcoming, safe space for people of every population and diversity — every race, ethnicity, gender identity, sexual orientation, faith background, age, and life experience. There is support out there. There is understanding and acceptance out there. Whoever you are and whatever brought you here, you'll find it in this practice.

You are not your struggle. You deserve care that meets you with respect, not shame.

Walking Alongside You

Recovery isn't meant to be a solo journey

No one should have to carry a mental health or addiction struggle in silence. Part of the role of this practice is simply to walk alongside you — consistently, patiently, and without judgment — while you rebuild routines, relationships, and a sense of stability at your own pace.

That support looks the same regardless of who you are: your race, ethnicity, gender identity, sexual orientation, faith, age, or life story. Everyone deserves a counselor who shows up fully for them.

Who We See

Children, adolescents & adults

Ages 5 and up — children, adolescents, teens, and adults dealing with substance use, anxiety, depression, trauma, and co-occurring mental health and addiction concerns, of every background, identity, and walk of life, across the Acadiana region and throughout Louisiana, entirely by telehealth.
