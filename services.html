Services

Focused support for mental health and addiction recovery

Care starts with individual counseling and grows with you — every session is structured around your goals and delivered entirely by secure telehealth, serving children ages 5 and up, adolescents, teens, and adults across the Acadiana region and throughout Louisiana. In-person visits are also coming soon. Every service here is offered without judgment, in a space that welcomes people of every background, identity, and diversity.

How Sessions Work

A humanistic, individualized approach — not a one-size-fits-all program

Every service below is delivered through a humanistic, client-centered lens: sessions start by meeting you exactly where you are, not where a treatment manual assumes you should be. From the first session, clinical assessment and treatment planning are built around your specific history, culture, goals, and pace — so the plan you leave with is yours alone, never a generic template applied the same way to everyone.

Areas of Focus

Types of counseling I provide

A closer look at the specific concerns sessions can address — each one delivered through the same humanistic, individualized lens, entirely by telehealth.

Mental Health Counseling

  • Anxiety and stress management
  • Depression and mood concerns
  • Trauma and PTSD
  • Grief and loss
  • Life transitions and major change
  • Self-esteem and personal growth

Addiction & Recovery Counseling

  • Substance use and addiction counseling
  • Co-occurring mental health and addiction concerns
  • Relapse prevention and coping-skills development
  • Structured, sustainable recovery planning

01 · Individual Counseling

One-on-one sessions built around your goals

Individual counseling addresses anxiety, depression, life transitions, and general mental health concerns through personalized assessment and treatment planning. Sessions are collaborative — you help set the pace and priorities. Early sessions focus on getting a full picture of your history, current stressors, and goals, so the plan that follows actually fits your life instead of a generic template.

  • Anxiety, depression & life stressors
  • Clinical assessment & personalized treatment planning
  • Delivered by secure telehealth, statewide

02 · Addiction & Recovery Support

Recovery-focused counseling, without judgment

Support for substance use and co-occurring mental health concerns, grounded in respect, structure, and hope. Care is designed to help clients build healthier routines and a more stable, sustainable recovery. Sessions may include relapse-prevention planning, coping-skills development, and coordination with medical or support-group resources when it's helpful — all shaped around where you are in your recovery, not a one-size-fits-all program.

  • Substance use & co-occurring disorder support
  • Structured, recovery-oriented sessions
  • Licensed Addiction Counselor (LAC) credentialed care

03 · Trauma-Informed Care

A steady space to process at your own pace

Trauma-informed sessions create a safe, predictable environment for working through past experiences and their impact on daily life, relationships, and well-being — never rushed, always at a pace that feels manageable. That means no session ever requires you to share more than you're ready to; the priority is building enough trust and safety that healing can happen without re-opening wounds before you're prepared to face them.

04 · Telehealth Counseling

The same care, from wherever you are

Secure video sessions bring counseling to clients across the Acadiana region and anywhere in Louisiana — ideal for those balancing work, caregiving, transportation, or simply preferring the privacy of home. Every session follows the same structure, confidentiality, and quality of care, session after session, so convenience never comes at the cost of connection.

  • Available across the Acadiana region and throughout Louisiana
  • Secure, HIPAA-compliant video platform
  • Backed by research as a genuinely effective way to receive care

What To Expect

A calm, unhurried space from the first hello

Every session happens over secure video, wherever you feel most at ease — but the goal is the same: a space where you can exhale, speak honestly, and be met without judgment. There's no dress code, no right way to show up, and no expectation that you'll have the answers before you log on.

Sessions are never recorded, and every appointment is held from a private, secure setting on this end too — the same confidentiality you'd expect from an office simply carries over to the screen.
