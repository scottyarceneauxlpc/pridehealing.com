Community Resources

Support beyond the counseling room

Counseling helps, and sometimes what you need most is a food pantry, a lawyer, a detox bed, a shelter, or someone to answer the phone at two in the morning. These are organizations serving Iberia Parish, Acadiana, and Louisiana, along with national lines that operate around the clock.

In An Emergency

If you are in danger right now, call 911

For a mental health crisis or thoughts of suicide, call or text 988 to reach the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, free and confidential, at any hour of any day. Veterans can dial 988 then press 1. For Spanish, press 2. You can also text HOME to 741741 to reach the Crisis Text Line.

This website and this practice do not provide emergency or after-hours crisis services. Messages sent here are not monitored around the clock.

About This List

A note on these listings

These organizations are listed to help you find support, and being listed here is not an endorsement, recommendation, or guarantee of the services any of them provide. Pride Healing & Recovery Services is not affiliated with them, does not control their services, and receives nothing for listing them.

Phone numbers, hours, and programs change. We check these periodically, but please confirm details directly with the organization. If you find something out of date or have a resource worth adding, let us know at admin@pridehealing.com and we will take a look.
